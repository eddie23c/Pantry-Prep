## Promotional text (170)

Get your home ready for a power cut, flood or storm. See how many days your food and water last, then fill the gaps with a simple shopping list.

## Description (4,000)

If the shops shut or the water stopped for a few days, would your household have enough? The UK Government's Be Ready advice says every home should keep food and water for at least three days. Pantry Prep makes that easy, calm and quick.

SEE HOW MANY DAYS YOU'RE COVERED
Tell Pantry Prep who lives with you and add what's in your cupboard. A single ring shows how many days your food and water last, plus water, protein, fibre and a balanced plate, so you know what to add.

ADD FOOD IN SECONDS
Search a list of store-cupboard staples, scan a barcode, or photograph your shelves. Pantry Prep counts the tins, packs and bottles for you.

A SHOPPING LIST THAT CLOSES EVERY GAP
Pantry Prep works out exactly what to buy next to reach three days, or seven, or fourteen.

MEALS WITHOUT POWER
No-cook and camping-stove recipes ranked by what you already hold, with filters for the 14 UK allergens and common diets.

PRINT CARDS FOR THE CUPBOARD
Print recipe cards and a dated stock list in large black-and-white type to keep with your food, so the plan still works when the phone is flat.

REMINDERS THAT KEEP IT FRESH
Get a nudge before food reaches its best-before date, and when it's time to check your torch, radio and batteries.

THE OFFICIAL BE READY CHECKLIST
Tick off the government's emergency kit list. Items in your store are ticked for you.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account. No adverts. No tracking. Your store is kept on your phone, and you can save a backup file whenever you like.

Pantry Prep follows UK Government Be Ready advice but is not a government service. It offers general guidance, not medical or dietary advice. Always check food labels for allergens.

## App Review Information

- Sign-in required: **No**.
- Contact: Eddie's name, phone and email (Apple only).
- Notes for the reviewer:

> Pantry Prep helps UK households build a three-day emergency food and water store, as UK Government Be Ready advice recommends. No account is needed and everything is stored on the device.
>
> To try it: tap Get started, then "Look around an example store first" to load a sample store. The Dashboard shows how many days the food lasts. Use + to add food by searching, scanning a barcode (try any tinned food) or photographing a shelf. The Recipes tab ranks no-cook meals by what is in the store. On the Prepare tab, "Download cards to print" opens the iOS print sheet; "Save a backup file" opens the share sheet. Adding a best-before date to an item asks permission for a reminder notification before that date.
>
> Native features: iOS print sheet (AirPrint/PDF), share sheet for backups, local notifications for best-before and kit-check reminders, haptic feedback, camera for barcodes and photos. Data is also mirrored to the app's own storage so it survives web view storage clean-up.
