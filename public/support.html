Pantry Prep support

Build a three-day food and water store for your home, following UK Government Be Ready advice.

Get in touch

Found a problem, or have an idea? Tell us here. Say what you were doing and which phone you use.

Where is my data kept?

Only on your phone. There are no accounts and no Pantry Prep server holding your store. Read the privacy notice.

How do I move my store to a new phone?

On the Prepare tab, choose Save a backup file and keep it somewhere safe, such as Files or iCloud Drive. On the new phone, choose Restore from a backup file.

How do I print the recipe cards?

On the Prepare tab, pick the recipes you want, then choose to print. On iPhone the print sheet opens: pick an AirPrint printer, or share it as a PDF to print elsewhere.

Why does the app ask to send notifications?

So it can remind you before food reaches its best-before date and when your emergency kit is due a check. The reminders are worked out on your phone. You can turn them off in Settings, Notifications, Pantry Prep.

How do I delete everything?

Remove items from the Store tab, or delete the app. Deleting the app removes everything it saved on your phone.

Is this official government advice?

No. Pantry Prep is based on the UK Government's Be Ready advice but is not a government service. In an emergency, follow instructions from the emergency services. See the terms and disclaimer.
